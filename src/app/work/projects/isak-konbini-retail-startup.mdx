## Overview

Co-founded ISAK Konbini, a convenience store startup at UWC ISAK Japan. Through data-driven product iteration and user research, we achieved a 389% return on investment while serving the school community's needs.

## Key Achievements

- **MVP Development**: Co-founded and launched a retail MVP from concept to operation
- **Data-Driven Optimization**: Used survey insights to optimize pricing, inventory, and user experience
- **Agile Iteration**: Ran weekly iteration cycles informed by user observation and feedback
- **Financial Success**: Achieved 389% ROI through strategic product decisions
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive tracking and documentation using Microsoft 365

## Product Management Approach

- Defined early product roadmap aligned with user needs and operational constraints
- Conducted regular user research through surveys and direct observation
- Implemented weekly iteration cycles to rapidly test and refine hypotheses
- Balanced user satisfaction with business sustainability

## Technologies & Tools

- MVP Design
- User Research
- Product Iteration
- Microsoft 365
- Financial Analysis
- Inventory Management

## Project Timeline

October 2022 - March 2024

## Challenges and Learnings

Operating a retail business taught valuable lessons about balancing competing priorities: user preferences, operational feasibility, and financial sustainability. The weekly iteration model allowed rapid testing of hypotheses while maintaining business continuity. Key learnings included the importance of data-driven decision making and the value of direct user observation in understanding behavior beyond stated preferences.

## Outcome

The venture successfully served the school community while achieving strong financial returns. The experience provided hands-on learning in product management, user research, financial management, and agile methodologies—skills that translate directly to tech product development.
