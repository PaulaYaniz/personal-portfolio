## Overview

Created an ArcGIS story map examining the humanitarian and disaster response efforts in Mozambique following Cyclone Idai. The project combines geospatial analysis with narrative storytelling to communicate complex disaster response patterns and resilience factors.

## Project Focus

- **Humanitarian Response**: Analysis of disaster response coordination and resource deployment
- **Resilience Patterns**: Examination of community resilience factors and recovery trajectories
- **Data Visualization**: Interactive mapping to illustrate impact zones and response efforts
- **Storytelling**: Narrative approach to communicate complex data to diverse audiences

## Technologies Used

- ArcGIS StoryMaps
- Geospatial Analysis
- Data Visualization
- GIS Mapping
- Data Storytelling

## Project Date

December 2024

## Key Insights

The analysis revealed critical patterns in disaster response effectiveness and identified key factors contributing to community resilience. The story map format allowed for accessible communication of complex geospatial data to policymakers, humanitarian organizations, and the general public.
