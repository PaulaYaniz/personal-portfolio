## Overview

AntiPhish is a native iOS app that teaches people how to recognize and avoid phishing attacks
through short lessons, quizzes, and real-world examples. I designed and built it end to end,
using Claude Code as an AI pair programmer to learn Swift from scratch and ship a working product.

## Key Achievements

- **Shipped from zero Swift experience**: Designed and shipped a full iOS app for phishing education, using Claude Code as an AI pair programmer to learn Swift and SwiftUI and accelerate development
- **Accessibility-first**: Implemented a full accessibility stack—VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, and text-to-speech—so the app works for users of all abilities
- **Product ownership**: Owned the app end to end, from defining the learning experience to structuring content and shipping the build

## Technologies & Skills

- Swift & SwiftUI
- AI pair programming (Claude Code)
- iOS accessibility (VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, TTS)
- Product design and content structuring

## Project Timeline

December 2025 - Present

## Challenges and Learnings

The core challenge was building a polished, accessible iOS app without prior Swift experience.
Pairing with Claude Code let me learn the language and framework in context—shipping real
features while understanding the code—rather than learning in isolation first. Designing for
accessibility from the start, rather than retrofitting it, kept the experience inclusive for
every user.

## Outcome

A working, accessibility-first iOS app that makes phishing education approachable, and a
demonstration of how AI-assisted development can compress the path from idea to shipped product.
