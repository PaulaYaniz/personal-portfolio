## Overview

Launched a digital voting platform for AV Raval, serving 10,000+ participants in a citywide art contest. The project focused on creating an accessible, user-friendly voting experience through comprehensive UX research and rapid prototyping.

## Key Achievements

- **User Research**: Conducted surveys and interviews to identify UX pain points and translate them into technical requirements
- **Project Management**: Created project roadmap and coordinated stakeholders to ensure on-time platform rollout
- **Product Development**: Translated user needs into a prioritized product backlog with clear technical specifications
- **Scale**: Successfully launched platform serving 10,000+ participants in a citywide art contest

## Technologies & Skills

- UX Research (surveys, interviews)
- Rapid Prototyping
- Product Roadmap Development
- Stakeholder Management
- Requirement Formation
- Feature Prioritization

## Project Timeline

May 2025 - July 2025

## Challenges and Learnings

The primary challenge was balancing diverse stakeholder needs with tight deadlines while ensuring the platform remained accessible to users with varying technical abilities. Through iterative user research and rapid prototyping, we identified critical pain points early and prioritized features that would have the most impact on user experience.

## Outcome

The platform successfully served over 10,000 participants in the citywide art contest, with positive feedback on usability and accessibility. The project demonstrated the value of user-centered design and agile development practices in delivering a product that meets both user needs and business objectives.
