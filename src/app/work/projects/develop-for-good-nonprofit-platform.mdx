## Overview

As Product Manager at Develop for Good, a 501(c)(3) that pairs volunteer teams with nonprofits,
I led a 10-person engineering and design team to launch a new website and internal database for
a nonprofit organization. I owned the product from discovery through launch.

## Key Achievements

- **Team leadership**: Led a 10-person engineering and design team from discovery to launch
- **Discovery & scoping**: Interviewed nonprofit leaders and IT admins to identify workflow bottlenecks and define the first product release
- **Execution**: Wrote PRDs and coordinated weekly sprints across engineering and design
- **Delivery**: Launched a Supabase/Vercel database and WordPress site serving 40+ customers

## Technologies & Skills

- Product roadmap and PRDs
- User interviews and stakeholder management
- Agile sprint planning across cross-functional teams
- Figma, Notion, Supabase, Vercel, WordPress

## Project Timeline

May 2026 - Present

## Challenges and Learnings

The main challenge was translating the varied needs of nonprofit staff—each with different
technical comfort levels—into a focused first release a volunteer team could ship on a fixed
timeline. Early interviews surfaced the real bottlenecks, which let me scope a backlog that
prioritized the highest-impact workflows and kept the team aligned sprint over sprint.

## Outcome

A launched website and internal database serving 40+ customers, delivered by a cross-functional
volunteer team through structured discovery, prioritization, and weekly delivery.
