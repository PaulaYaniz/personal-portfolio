## 💡 Tips

1. **Size matters**: Keep images under 500KB for fast loading
2. **Format**: Use JPG for photos, PNG for screenshots with text
3. **Aspect ratio**: Project covers work best at 16:9 (e.g., 1600x900px)
4. **Naming**: Keep filenames descriptive and lowercase with hyphens (e.g., `av-raval-dashboard.jpg`)

---

## ❓ Need Help?

To replace an image:
1. Download your image from paulayaniz.github.io or take a new screenshot
2. Go to the GitHub repository
3. Navigate to the image location (e.g., `public/images/projects/project-01/`)
4. Click "Add file" → "Upload files"
5. Drag your image and name it exactly as specified above
6. Commit the changes

Vercel will automatically redeploy with your new images!
